Changes from the originals
--------------------------

Each file was taken from the pack's "PNG (Transparent)" set, resized from
512x512 with Lanczos resampling, and converted to white RGB with the shape
carried entirely in alpha, so that a Pixi tint reproduces the arena's theme
colour exactly rather than a darker cast of it.
